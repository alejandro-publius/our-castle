# PayPal hillside lettering

`paypal-wordmark.svg` is the unmodified wordmark served by PayPal at https://www.paypalobjects.com/paypal-ui/logos/svg/paypal-wordmark-color.svg , referenced by https://www.paypal.com/us/home and retrieved November 5, 2026 Pacific.

SHA-256: `f766ba6d9471acc787c0808b8c30f38494d12b287ccfb2ff610fd617cfa2a432`.

`wordmark-shapes.js` samples those same six outlined glyphs and their four holes at a maximum segment spacing of 0.38 SVG units, for solid 3D geometry. It preserves the relative lettering and spacing; no font is downloaded, approximated or redistributed. The game renders the letter faces white with shaded extrusion and support posts.
