# Market Town tavern

The owner's miniature-tavern request and the existing Market Town cottage are the primary reference lock. Keep the original red-roof, blue-shuttered market house and its household banner; add a modest cream/crimson porch awning, carved tankard sign, two barrels, one outdoor table, two benches, bread, mugs and a warm lantern. The established Refero Wayfinder direction contributes only restrained world-first presentation. No imported imagery, new fonts, interface panels or text labels.

Two seated neighbors and an innkeeper form one small gathering on the west side of the market, away from the well, stalls, windmill and central approach. They are background townsfolk, separate from the unchanged twenty-person earned court. The tavern inherits Market Town's existing district access; it adds no milestone, financial credit or guest entitlement. Original houses and neighboring features remain.

Heads turn and mugs lift occasionally using the existing ambient clock. Static furnishing/body meshes join the town's instance batches; only heads and gesture arms stay articulated. Reduced motion is still; hidden tabs pause; resumed time is capped. Rebuilding clears old figure references before recreating the same three townsfolk. No extra timer or provider request is added.
